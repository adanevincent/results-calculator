JHS Results Calculator

A web-based educational tool designed to help Junior High School (JHS) students, parents, and teachers calculate examination results and better understand students' academic performance.

The project aims to simplify results calculation and provide useful academic information to support school selection and educational planning.

 Project Objectives

* Simplify the calculation of JHS examination results.
* Help students and parents understand academic performance.
* Provide information that can support school-selection decisions.
* Make results calculation more accessible through a web-based platform.

 Planned Features

* Results Calculation: Calculate examination results based on the information entered by the user.
* School Category Information: Help users explore school categories relevant to their results.
* Simple User Interface: Provide an easy-to-use interface for students and parents.
* Results Access:Explore ways for users to access their previously saved calculations.

Note: Features that are still under development may not yet be available.

Project Vision

The long-term goal is to develop a practical educational platform that makes results calculation and school-selection information easier to access.

The project is being developed with a focus on usability, affordability, and solving real problems faced by students and parents.

 Project Status
